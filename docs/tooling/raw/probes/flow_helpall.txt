Usage: flow [OPTIONS] [ECL_DECK_FILENAME]
Flow, an advanced reservoir simulator for ECL-decks provided by the Open Porous Media project.

Recognized options:

Available options are normal (do not apply keywords that have not been tested for ActionX or PyAction) and low (try to apply all keywords, beware: the simulation outcome might be incorrect). Default: "normal"
