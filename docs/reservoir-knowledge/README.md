# Reservoir Knowledge & Validation Context

This workstream captures the minimum reservoir-domain knowledge required to design and interpret AURORA responsibly. Its purpose is not to turn the software team into reservoir engineers; it is to prevent incorrect variable interpretation, undocumented physical assumptions, meaningless constraints, and unsupported claims.

Topics expected here include porosity, permeability, pressure, saturation, phases, porous-media flow, producers/injectors, bottom-hole pressure, rates and controls, waterflooding, reservoir simulation, reduced-order modeling, and the physical interpretation of AURORA's observations/actions/objectives/constraints.

Domain-sensitive statements should cite authoritative documentation, textbooks, peer-reviewed literature, benchmark documentation, or qualified expert review. Unknowns should be marked `TBD — SOURCE REQUIRED` or `TBD — EXPERT REVIEW`.
