\section{Verification and Experimental Methodology}
\label{sec:experiments}

AURORA's experimental methodology is designed around traceability from configuration
to claim. Important runs will identify the reservoir realization, simulator
environment, controller and safety configurations, random seeds where applicable,
control steps, observations, proposed actions, applied actions, interventions,
failures, and summary metrics.

Comparisons will define an explicit baseline, changed factor, held-constant factors,
metric, aggregation method, uncertainty treatment, and scope of conclusion. Failed or
invalid runs relevant to the evaluation will not be silently discarded; their status
and exclusion rationale will be retained.

The final evaluation protocol will distinguish development or calibration conditions
from protected evaluation conditions where scientifically appropriate. Exact
realization splits, controller observations/actions, objective formulation,
operational constraints, mismatch-calibration method, and numerical success
thresholds remain controlled design decisions and are not frozen by the present
proposal foundation.

AURORA further distinguishes statistical significance, engineering significance,
physical significance, and safety significance. A statistically unusual result is not
automatically physically concerning, and a physically concerning numerical result is
not automatically an encoded safety violation. Each interpretation must follow the
definitions and evidence established for the corresponding experiment.
